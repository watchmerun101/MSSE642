# AI Slop Discussion
This topic is a major talking point regarding any job it seems like these days. As Professor Randall mentioned, the churn of "Discussion Post" and "Generic Assignment" to obtain the "knowledge" to get a professional degree has degraded the worth of the degree itself. I say this because new hires at my current job seem to lack a lot of the problem solving and knowledge that they should of obtained in their early engineering. This is most relevant when we were working on a new product launch (vehicle) with a large team and we assigned a new engineer to collect data, analyze data, and present the data. This engineer collected the requested data but when working on analysis they were asked to calculate the Cp and graph the data for presentation. Instead of researching the calculation, they requested me to help (do it for them) to complete half of their task. I find that most people don't seem to know how to take a task/problem and create a plan, action, and execution without guidance. This may stem from the need to use AI to create step by step guides or execution of a problem instead of problem solving themselves.
